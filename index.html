ACTIVATION

Activate Windows & Office

Installing is only half the job — here's how to confirm you're properly licensed and fix the most common activation errors.

Activate Windows

  1. Open Settings and go to System > Activation.
  2. Click Change product key and enter your 25-character key.
  3. Click Next and follow the prompts — activation happens automatically once verified.
  4. Recheck the Activation page any time — it should read "Windows is activated."

Activate Office

  1. Open any Office app, like Word.
  2. If prompted, sign in with the Microsoft account linked to your license.
  3. If not prompted, go to File > Account and check Product Information.
  4. Click Activate Product if shown, and sign in when asked.

"We can't activate Windows on this device"

  1. Make sure you're connected to the internet — activation reaches Microsoft's servers.
  2. Run the built-in Activation Troubleshooter on the Activation settings page.
  3. Double-check your key has no typos — 0 (zero) and O (letter) look alike.
  4. Still stuck? The key may be tied to different hardware — contact wherever you got the license.

Office keeps asking to activate

  1. Confirm you're signed in with the correct account — check File > Account.
  2. Sign out completely, restart the app, and sign back in.
  3. Make sure your subscription or license hasn't expired.
  4. Signing in on too many devices for one license can trigger this.
Quick check: Press Win + I to open Settings, then go to System → Activation. It clearly says "Windows is activated" or lists an error you can search by name.
